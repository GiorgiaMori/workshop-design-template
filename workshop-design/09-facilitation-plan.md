# Facilitation Plan

Define facilitation roles, prompts, and contingency plans.

## Role assignments

### Trainer
- Leads content delivery
- Runs demos
- Monitors pacing

### Facilitator
- Monitors questions/chat
- Supports exercises
- Captures common blockers

### Host
- Manages platform logistics
- Handles breakout rooms/polls
- Coordinates recording and communication
