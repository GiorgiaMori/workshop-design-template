# Nanopore Genome Assembly Example

This example shows a complete run through the BioCommons Workshop Designer pathway for a 6-hour workshop on bacterial long-read assembly.

## What to look for

- Learner-first scoping in step 1
- Audience constraints and prerequisites in step 2
- Brain dump and parking lot triage in steps 3-4
- Measurable outcomes and realism checks in steps 5-6
- Exercise and demo mapping in steps 7-8
- Accessibility, delivery readiness, and reflection in steps 9-12
