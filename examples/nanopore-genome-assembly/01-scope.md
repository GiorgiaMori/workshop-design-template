# Scope (Example)

## Learner problem statement

Participants are trying to solve this real-world problem:

"I have Oxford Nanopore reads from a bacterial isolate and need to produce and assess a draft genome assembly for downstream comparative analysis."

## Workshop topic

Practical long-read bacterial genome assembly and quality assessment.

## In scope

- Inspect FASTQ read quality and summary metrics
- Assemble reads with Flye
- Assess assembly quality with QUAST and BUSCO (bacteria profile)
- Interpret key quality metrics for go/no-go decisions

## Out of scope

- Hybrid assembly with short reads
- Polishing with multiple rounds and advanced parameter tuning
- Eukaryotic genome assembly
- Phylogenomics workflows

## Constraints

- Delivery length: 6 hours
- Delivery mode: online (live, facilitator-supported)
- Platform constraints: pre-provisioned Linux environment; no local admin rights required
- Team constraints: 1 trainer, 2 facilitators
